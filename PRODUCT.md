# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People seeking country-level data and statistics about Western first-world countries, with an emphasis on indicators that affect everyday life for the common native individual.

## Product Purpose

Project WatchTower brings regularly updated country data and statistics into one intelligence atlas. Success means a visitor can move from a global overview to a country dossier and understand relevant demographic, economic, governmental, social, health, crime, and migration indicators.

## Positioning

WatchTower combines a Western-first-world geographic scope with an individual-impact lens: it prioritizes country indicators according to their relevance to ordinary life rather than presenting a general-purpose global database.

## Operating Context

Visitors enter through a welcome page, explore coverage through either an interactive globe or a country-flag gallery, and open individual country dossiers. The interface supports mouse, touch, keyboard navigation, search, and shareable country routes.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Interactive globe and country-flag views provide two ways to browse coverage.
- Country dossiers aggregate statistics and explanatory material across multiple indicator categories.
- Data will be updated regularly for relevance.
- The project is a work in progress; incomplete coverage and issues are expected.
- Not every country currently has a live dossier.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: Project WatchTower.
- Primary proposition: “Western-first-world country intelligence.”
- Welcome-page mission line: “Global Reconnaissance.”
- Voice: direct, factual, restrained, and transparent about incomplete coverage.

## Evidence on Hand

- Country, demographic, economic, government, health, crime, migration, and military datasets under `src/data`, `src/lib`, and `Assets/Data`.
- Country flags under `Assets/Flags`.
- Europe cartographic imagery under `public/hero` and `Assets`.
- Interactive globe geometry under `src/data/worldLand.ts` and `src/data/countryShapes.ts`.
- No testimonials, customer logos, external benchmarks, or completion claims are available and none should be fabricated.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with country facts that matter to everyday life.
2. Make geographic coverage and dossier availability immediately legible.
3. Keep data current and distinguish live coverage from work in progress.
4. Let visitors move quickly from global context to country-level detail.
5. Prefer evidence and transparent status over promotional claims.
